The Ultimate Visitor Guide to Cloudcroft, New Mexico

Essential Cloudcroft

The Mexican Canyon Trestle

A towering monument to the "Cloud-Climbing Railroad." Best viewed from the Trestle Recreation Area trails.

The Lodge at Cloudcroft

Visit the historic 1899 hotel, home to the famous Rebecca's restaurant and a resident ghost.

High-Altitude Hiking

Explore the Osha Trail or the Rim Trail for breathtaking views of White Sands National Park.

Dining & Legend

Mad Jack’s Mountaintop BBQ

Authentic Lockhart-style BBQ. Tip: The line starts at 9:00 AM, and they sell out fast!

Winter Wonderland

Ski Cloudcroft

Southernmost skiing in NM. Perfect for families and beginners.

Tubing & Skating

Experience the James Sewell Ice Rink or hit the tubing hills at Ski Cloudcroft.
