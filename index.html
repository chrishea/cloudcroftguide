A Destination for Every Season

At 9,000 feet in the Sacramento Mountains, Cloudcroft offers a true four-season escape from the desert heat below

Spring

Highs 50-65°F

Wildflowers bloom across meadows. Trails emerge from winter snow. Perfect for birdwatching and photography.

Summer

Highs 70-80°F

Escape the desert heat. Cool mountain air, afternoon monsoons, and prime hiking conditions.

Fall

Highs 55-70°F

Aspens turn gold against evergreen pines. Crisp air, harvest festivals, and stunning scenic drives.

Winter

Highs 40-50°F

Snow blankets the mountains. Ski at the southernmost slopes in America. Cozy cabins and warm fires.

Things to Do

From alpine adventures to starlit nights, discover what makes Cloudcroft unforgettable

🥾

Hiking & Trails

Explore miles of pristine trails through ponderosa pines and aspen groves in the Lincoln National Forest. From easy nature walks to challenging mountain ascents.

⛷️

Skiing & Winter Sports

Hit the slopes at the southernmost ski area in America. Family-friendly terrain with stunning views of White Sands below.

🚗

Scenic Drives

Wind through spectacular mountain passes on the Sunspot Scenic Byway. Breathtaking overlooks and access to the National Solar Observatory.

🦌

Wildlife Viewing

Spot elk, mule deer, wild turkeys, and countless bird species. The Sacramento Mountains are home to diverse mountain wildlife.

⭐

Stargazing

Experience some of the darkest skies in New Mexico. High altitude and clear mountain air create perfect conditions for astronomy.

🏛️

Historic Sites

Explore Victorian-era architecture, railroad heritage, and the storied past of this mountain resort town established in 1899.

Where to Stay

Find your perfect mountain retreat among the pines

Historic Lodges

Experience Victorian elegance at The Lodge Resort, built in 1899. Fine dining, golf, spa services, and timeless mountain hospitality.

Cabins & Rentals

Cozy cabins nestled in the pines offer privacy and comfort. Wood-burning fireplaces, mountain views, perfect for families or romantic getaways.

Camping & RV

Pitch your tent or park your RV among towering ponderosas. Multiple campgrounds in the Lincoln National Forest with mountain access.

Mountain Dining

From BBQ to bakeries, craft brewing to fine dining—Cloudcroft's food scene punches above its weight

Featured Local Spots

Levain It Up Sourdough Bakery

High Rolls

Artisan sourdough with starters over 130 years old. Croissants with 27 layers, breads requiring 8 days from start to finish. Open Friday-Sunday.

Cloudcroft Brewing Company

1301 Burro Ave. | (575) 682-2337

High-altitude craft beer, wood-fired pizza, live music. Open 11am-9pm (10pm Fri-Sat). Closed Tuesdays.

Mad Jack's Mountaintop BBQ

True Texas-Style BBQ

Award-winning smokehouse drawing weekend crowds. One of New Mexico's most celebrated barbecue destinations.

Visitor Information

Everything you need to plan your mountain getaway

📍

Getting Here

Located on US Highway 82, just 16 miles east of Alamogordo. 85 miles from Las Cruces, 90 miles from El Paso, and 200 miles from Albuquerque.

🏔️

Elevation

8,676 - 9,200 feet above sea level. Pack layers—temperatures can drop 20-30 degrees from the valley below.

🌡️

Weather

Summer highs 70-80°F, winter highs 40-50°F. Afternoon thunderstorms common July-September. Snow possible October-April.

🌲

Lincoln National Forest

Cloudcroft sits within the Lincoln National Forest. Permits required for some activities. Ranger station located in town.

📶

Connectivity

Cell service varies by carrier. Many lodges offer WiFi. Embrace the opportunity to disconnect in the mountains.

🐕

Pet Friendly

Many trails, lodges, and restaurants welcome well-behaved pets. Keep dogs leashed in the National Forest.
