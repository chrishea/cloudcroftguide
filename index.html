Things to Do

From alpine adventures to starlit nights, discover what makes Cloudcroft unforgettable

🥾

Hiking & Trails

Explore miles of pristine trails through ponderosa pines and aspen groves. From easy nature walks to challenging mountain ascents, there's a path for every adventurer.

⛷️

Skiing & Winter Sports

Hit the slopes at southernmost ski resort in the U.S. Perfect for families and beginners, with stunning views of White Sands below.

🚗

Scenic Drives

Wind through spectacular mountain passes and discover breathtaking overlooks. The Sunspot Scenic Byway offers unforgettable vistas.

🦌

Wildlife Viewing

Spot elk, deer, wild turkeys, and countless bird species in their natural habitat. Bring binoculars for the best experience.

⭐

Stargazing

Experience some of the darkest skies in New Mexico. The high altitude and clear air create perfect conditions for astronomy.

🏛️

Historic Sites

Explore Victorian-era architecture, railroad heritage, and the storied past of this mountain resort town established in 1899.

Where to Stay

Find your perfect mountain retreat

Historic Lodges

Experience Victorian elegance at The Lodge Resort, built in 1899. Enjoy fine dining, golf, and timeless mountain hospitality.

Cabins & Rentals

Cozy cabins nestled in the pines offer privacy and comfort. Perfect for families or romantic getaways.

Camping & RV

Pitch your tent or park your RV among towering ponderosas. Multiple campgrounds offer mountain access and amenities.

Mountain Dining

From BBQ to bakeries, brewing to fine dining—Cloudcroft's food scene has something for everyone

Featured Local Stories

Levain It Up Sourdough Bakery

High Rolls

Garrett Barnett and Steven Jackson are transforming lives through artisan sourdough. Their starters—Queen (130+ years old), Doughlores, Matildough, and Doughlene—create croissants with 27 layers and breads requiring 8 days from start to finish. Open Friday-Sunday.

Cloudcroft Brewing Company

1301 Burro Ave. | (575) 682-2337

Brewmaster Elliot Bell transforms local ingredients into beers that tell the story of our high-altitude home. Wood-fired pizza, soup du jour, salads, and fantastic atmosphere with live music. Open 11:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. (10:00 p.m. Friday-Saturday). Closed Tuesdays.

Mad Jack's Mountaintop BBQ

True Texas-Style BBQ

James Jackson's acclaimed smokehouse draws long weekend lines. Now expanding to Johnson City, Texas, while keeping the Cloudcroft location thriving. One of New Mexico's most celebrated barbecue destinations.
